A little about me

Outside of coursework I’ve taken part in math olympiads and play CTFs (capture-the-flag security competitions). I like problems with a clean answer that still has to be found.

I’m looking for an MS or PhD programme where I can keep doing research in NLP, LLMs, retrieval and AI agents. I want to work with people who care about clean systems, thoughtful experiments, and things that are useful after the demo is over.

Hobbies

Games & chess

I like video games with places that feel lived in. Rusty machinery, quiet corners, and a world that looks like someone drew every little thing by hand. I also play chess competitively, have won some online chess tournaments, and rank in the top 1% on Chess.com puzzles.

Art & drawing

I like drawing, old illustrations, folk-print textures, uneven lines, and artwork that still looks as if a person made it.

Books & music

I read when I can, and there is usually music somewhere in the background. I have a soft spot for work with a point of view and a few rough edges left in. I’m also learning to play guitar, and I can already play a few songs.

Small collections

I collect keychains and currency. Tiny souvenirs, odd designs, and coins and notes that have travelled through lives I’ll never know about.
